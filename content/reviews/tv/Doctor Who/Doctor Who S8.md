And *away* we go!

Suddenly, Twelve! Capaldi is such a different feeling doctor from either Ten or Eleven. It's really jarring to get into, but as his seasons go on, I really do think he's right up there with all of them for bests. 

And at least to tide us over, we still have Clara--now *finally* past her arc as the Impossible Girl. 

Overall, this is quite the solid season. There are a few gems and I do love the building mystery and eventual reveals around Missy. It's not as huge a season arc as we've had, but it's still a solid one. 

*Deep Breath* brings back the Paternoster Gang (always nice) and introduces Missy. 

*Into the Dalek* introduces Danny for better or for worse, and gives us another iteration of a 'good' Dalek. 

*Robot of Sherwood* gives us Robin Hood. Which, for some reason, the Doctor refuses to believe is real. That's a funny one. And I do love Doctor Who's take on the archery contest. 

*Listen* is a weird take on the monster under the bed and time travel. Timey whimey causal loops et al. 

*Time Heist* is a lot of fun. One of my favorites this season. The perfect bank--the perfect heist. With a crew that has to have their memories wiped in order to even get into the door...

*The Caretaker*. Clara trying to live two lives--and the Doctor barging in. 

*Kill the Moon*, which is ... just kind of weird? How in the world would the egg being a moon work with *any* other episode really.

*Mummy on the Orient Express* is just excellent. Murder mystery done Doctor Who style. My other favorite this season. 

*Flatline* which is funny because the Tardis gets even *smaller* on the outside. And creepy in analogies to flatland. 

*Dark Water* / *Death in Heaven*--where Missy is revealed, death has never been the answer, and a main character finds out about an afterlife first hand.

Onward to season 9!
