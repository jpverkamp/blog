The one about Athena/Annabeth. 

We have the crew of the *Argo II* on their way to where it all began in Rome/Greece. And if that wasn't enough, Annabeth has been given the titular *Mark of Athena*. And when anyone in universe hears about this, there are gasps and sideeyes, so you know that's not going to be anything good. 

Other than that, we do get the crew of demigods on the Argo II together. I love the dynamic of having so many of them, different in all their different ways. It's kind of weird having such an Annabeth centric story. Have we ever had that? 

Still, another fine story in this ever escalating 'can we get *every* Greek and Roman myth into the universe before we run out of books' series. :smile:

And finally--that *ending*. 

Oh cliffhangers. I'm *so* glad that whole series is out already.
