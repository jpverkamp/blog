What is this? New Dresden Files?! Count me in!

Twelve Months takes place in the year (heh) after Peace Talks and Battle Ground. And in a lot of ways, it's a book about healing and moving on. Which, after the absolute *mess* that Battle Ground made of Chicago in general and Dresden in particular? 

> “A wizard is never late,” I muttered. “He arrives as soon as he’s dealt with his PTSD.” 

Yeah. Dresden needed this book. *We* needed this book. 

Of course, that doesn't mean things are ever going to be quiet. In addition to recovering, we still have a city without powers, dark things prowling the streets, wheeling and dealing between the Accorded Nations and Thomas is still locked up. Oh. And Dresden is still engaged to Lara Raith--so it's about time they started courting. 

I love seeing Dresden grow. Not just in power--although he's done a lot of that--but as a person. He's been through *so much* with so many of the other characters of this series. And seeing them grow and change throughout it is awesome. I especially like seeing the scenes between Dresden/Mab and Dresden/Lara. Which I suppose is *exactly* the reason this book exists in the first place. 

> “Stupidity,” Mab mused. “Courage. The only difference is the outcome.” 

I'm looking forward to what comes next--especially as it's supposed to be the long awaited *Mirror Mirror*. With 18 books now though, the reread/relisten is getting to be a lot. Worth it though!
