I think I've seen this movie before? If not, I'm sure I've read the book. But it's been a long time either way.

Overall, it's a fun enough movie. It's pretty much the stereotypical story of 'kid is bullied by (way over the top) adults / kid is right / kid ends up winning the day'. Only with a touch of unexplained (in the movie version) psychic powers (which is fun) in the mix.

It's a fun movie. 

It's still really hard to watch Trunchbull. Which I suppose is entirely the point. To a lesser extent the Wormwood family dynamics--that part at least is depressingly believable. 

Onward!
