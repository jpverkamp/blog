> Yelena Belova: There's something wrong with me. An emptiness. I thought it started when my sister died, but now it feels like something bigger. Just a void. Or maybe I'm just bored.

The [[Marvel Cinematic Universe]]() has been a little rough for a while. A few good movies, but mostly, everything mostly feels by the book.

Thunderbolts*... is a *surprisingly* good movie. 

It could have been forgettable. More action movie/big skybeam showdown/forgettable mirror villains. It could have been Marvel's answer to [[The Suicide Squad]]() (either really). 

But somehow, it's better than that? 

Basically, we take a who's who of the forgotten broken anti-heroes (or even outright villains), each sent on a mission to solve the 'problems' the others pose. Florence Pugh's Yelena is the heart of the movie, but David Harbour's Red Guardian is a lot of fun, Wyatt Russell's John Walker is ... better than he was in [[The Falcon and the Winter Soldier]]. We have a Ghost and a Taskmaster. 

And Bob. 

It's kind of cool seeing a new team come together. They're not the Avengers*, but somehow it works. 

And they have a frankly terrifying villain to contend with--themselves. 

Oh. And there's Bob. 

I have no idea if this will work in the greater cinematic universe, but that's okay. 

They're a bunch of not great people that have honestly done some terrible things. And I'm not sure we can just forgive that. 

But it's a fun movie, it's got a point, and I'm so glad that Marvel can still sometimes put something out like this.
