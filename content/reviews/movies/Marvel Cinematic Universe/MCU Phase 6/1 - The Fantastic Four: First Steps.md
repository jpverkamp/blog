How many Fantastic Four movies is that, just since 200? Four? 

I think I've actually seen them all. One even had a sequel And I'm not sure I can remember anything from any of them really. 

This one has a nice cast, a fun, well done retro-futuristic aesthetic, found family, and some future/world building potential. It's not the best MCU movie by any standard, but I think it might just be the best Fantastic Four film. 

The casting was well done. Pedro Pascal actually plays a pretty smart Reed Richards, which can be hard to pull off. Vanessa Kirby and Joseph Quinn pull off siblings well, and I enjoyed Ebon Moss-Bachrach's Ben Grimm. 

Having Galactus as the villain, and then turning him into an admittedly giant of a monster, but nowhere near planet eating scale works. How do you fight something the size of a planet? The size of a skyscraper, that works.
